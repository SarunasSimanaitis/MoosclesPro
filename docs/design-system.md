MoosclesPro Design Philosophy
🌑 Calm

Dark charcoal, not pure black.

Comfortable to look at for long workout sessions.

⚪ Spacious

Whitespace is intentional.

Not every pixel needs content.

🟣 Focused

One accent color.

Indigo.

If everything is important...

Nothing is.

✨ Smooth

Every animation has a purpose.

No flashy effects.

Just fluid interactions.

🎯 Motivating

The app never shouts.

It gently nudges.

Instead of

MISSING WORKOUT!!

It says

Two workouts left this week.

💪 Built for consistency

We reward showing up.

Not perfection.
